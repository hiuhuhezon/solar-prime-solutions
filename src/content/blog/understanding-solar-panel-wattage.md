One of the most common questions we get from customers is: "How many solar panels do I actually need?" The answer comes down to a simple calculation once you know your daily energy usage.

## The basic formula

```
Daily energy need (kWh) ÷ Peak sun hours ÷ Panel wattage = Number of panels needed
```

## A worked example

Let's say a household uses **10 kWh per day**, and your location in Kenya gets an average of **5 peak sun hours** per day. If you're using **400W panels**, here's the calculation:

```
10,000 Wh ÷ 5 hours ÷ 400W ≈ 5 panels
```

That gives you a starting estimate of **5 panels** to cover daily usage under ideal conditions. In practice, we usually recommend adding a 20-30% buffer for cloudy days and system losses, so a real-world recommendation might land closer to 6-7 panels.

## Why this matters

Undersizing a system means running out of power on cloudy days or at night without enough battery backup. Oversizing wastes money on panels you don't need. Getting this number right — combined with the right battery capacity — is the foundation of a solar system that actually meets your needs.
